Digital Talent Passport

Unlocking Displaced Talent | Kenya

A verified record of who you are and what you can do.

Digital Talent Passport is a web-based platform designed to address the challenge of credential recognition and skills validation among skilled refugees in Kenya.

Many displaced professionals possess academic qualifications, technical abilities and years of work experience. However, missing documents, difficulties verifying foreign qualifications and limited access to recognition systems can prevent them from accessing opportunities matching their abilities.

Digital Talent Passport provides a practical digital pathway for candidates to create professional profiles, submit credentials, complete skills assessments and generate digital professional passports.

The Problem

Skilled refugees often experience brain waste: their qualifications and professional abilities remain underutilized because employers and institutions cannot easily assess or recognize their credentials.

This project focuses on making professional skills and qualifications easier to present, assess and review.

Our Solution

Digital Talent Passport provides a centralized digital platform where skilled refugees can:

- Create and manage professional profiles.
- Upload academic and professional certificates.
- Request credential reviews.
- Complete skills assessments.
- View assessment results.
- Generate and download digital Talent Passports.
- Present their professional information in a structured digital format.

Key Features

1. Home Page

Introduces the platform, its mission and how digital talent recognition can help unlock displaced talent.

2. Professional Profile

Allows candidates to create and save professional profiles containing their education, experience and professional category.

3. Credential Submission

Allows candidates to upload certificates and request credential reviews. Submitted credentials have a review status, including Pending Review.

4. Skills Assessment

Provides a skills assessment with automatic scoring and assessment results. Candidates who achieve the required score can pass the assessment.

5. Digital Talent Passport

Generates a professional digital passport displaying candidate information, assessment status and a QR code. Candidates can download their passport as a PDF.

6. Impact Dashboard

Displays platform statistics, including:

- Registered talents.
- Completed assessments.
- Verified credentials.
- Bridging recommendations.
- Passports issued.

7. SDG Alignment

- SDG 4 – Quality Education: Recognizing skills and supporting pathways for further learning and certification.
- SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth: Helping skilled refugees demonstrate their abilities and improve access to economic opportunities.
